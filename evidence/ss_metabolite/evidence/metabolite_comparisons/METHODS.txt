Expression: arithmetic means of three DESeq2-normalized biological replicates in each of three groups; row z-score across all three means, population SD (ddof=0), no log transformation. Zero-variance genes are grey; display clipped at ±2.5, source tables unclipped. Metabolites: relative abundances, untransformed replicate dot plots, mean ± sample SD; no pseudocount or inferred detection-limit replacement. Independent cohorts preclude sample-level gene–metabolite correlations. Amino acids/related compounds and ambiguous duplicated columns excluded with audit. Six predefined organic acids used without selection based on observed significance. Tissue contrasts: absolute difference of arithmetic means, all 84 group-label allocations, two-sided exact randomization test under exchangeability, BH adjustment across six tests. Pooled metabolite correlations: Spearman, descriptive only. Tissue-adjusted correlations: Pearson correlations of global ranks after subtracting within-tissue rank means; exact permutation of one variable within each tissue (4320 permutations), two-sided absolute statistic; BH across estimable pairs. These exploratory results are limited by n=9, unconfirmed preprocessing/detection floors, and tissue/cohort effects. No night metabolomics, flux inference, or gene-function validation. Same metabolite panel repeated for each family is contextual, not a family-specific target assignment. No RNA-seq differential testing is performed. Metabolite cohort label permutation does not remove unrecorded batch effects. Correlation adjustment removes tissue intercepts only, not all confounding.
