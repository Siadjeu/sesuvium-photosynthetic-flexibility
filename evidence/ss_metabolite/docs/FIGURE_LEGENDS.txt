Final transcript–metabolite comparison figures

Daytime leaf-versus-cotyledon comparison in S. sesuvioides. (A) Existing apeglm-shrunken gene-expression log2 fold changes for supported family members. Positive/red effects indicate higher expression in leaves; negative/blue effects indicate higher expression in cotyledons. Filled dots meet the original primary-DE criteria (within-contrast adjusted p < 0.05 and absolute shrunken log2 fold change >= 1); open dots fail at least one criterion. Labels give adjusted p-values, displayed as q. Unassessable genes are omitted from plots but retained in the evidence tables; titles report plotted versus total supported loci. (B) Log2 ratios of mean metabolite relative abundance in leaves (n=6 independent plants) versus cotyledons (n=3), both daytime. Asterisks indicate BH-adjusted p < 0.05 across six metabolite comparisons; ns indicates adjusted p >= 0.05. Tests used all 84 group-label allocations of the absolute mean difference. Reaction-associated panels use documented family-level substrate/product relationships; context-only panels do not assert a direct relationship. The two assays used different plants. The panels therefore show condition-level comparisons, not sample-level correlations. Malate estimates may be affected by a preprocessing floor.

Supporting combined expression/metabolite figures

(A) Row z-scores of arithmetic mean normalized expression across leaf day, cotyledon day and cotyledon night, using three RNA-seq biological replicates per group. Z-scores were calculated across the three group means using population SD, without log transformation; display limits are +/-2.5, and constant rows are grey. (B) Daytime metabolite relative abundances, individual biological replicates and mean +/- SD. Comparison brackets give BH-adjusted p-values across six metabolites. (C) Pooled Spearman correlations across nine metabolomics samples. (D) Correlations of global ranks after subtracting within-tissue rank means. Exact within-tissue permutation p-values and BH-adjusted values across 15 pairs are provided in the accompanying table. None passed adjusted p < 0.05. The common metabolite panel is contextual and does not imply that every family directly acts on every displayed metabolite.
