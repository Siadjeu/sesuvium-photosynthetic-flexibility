Interpretation and reporting limits

1. RNA-seq and metabolomics were measured in different plants. No sample-level gene–metabolite correlation is estimable. Only two overlapping conditions are available, so correlating their two means would be uninformative.
2. Metabolomics includes daytime samples only. The data cannot test diel acid accumulation or demonstrate CAM cycling.
3. Metabolite relative abundances do not measure enzyme activity or metabolic flux. Pool changes can reflect production, consumption, transport and compartmentation. Neither same-sign nor opposite-sign changes establish a gene's role.
4. There are only three cotyledon and six leaf metabolomics samples. Statistical power is limited; lack of FDR significance does not demonstrate absence of an effect. Exact permutation p-values are discrete. Tissue adjustment removes group mean ranks but not unknown confounders or differences in variance.
5. Laboratory normalization, detection-limit replacement, acquisition method and identification confidence remain incompletely documented. Repeated low values may reflect preprocessing. Their origin must be clarified; they were not silently reinterpreted as concentrations or missing values.
6. Duplicate metabolite names and identical columns were excluded and recorded. Results concern a predefined six-organic-acid panel, not a comprehensive metabolome survey. Amino acids were intentionally excluded.
7. Family-level reaction mapping is not locus-specific biochemical validation. Transporter/regulator figures labeled context-only do not claim direct catalytic relationships. Compartmentation and cofactor specificity require independent evidence.
8. Sixteen unassessable genes remain in the ledger. Their omission from plots does not imply absence, lack of expression or lack of biological importance.
9. RNA-seq shrunken log2 fold changes and metabolite log2 mean ratios are different estimands. Their magnitudes should not be equated; statistical adjustments apply to separate test sets.
10. This is a computational evidence freeze, not a declaration that the experimental methods are publication-complete. The laboratory metadata gap remains explicitly open. No GitHub publication is performed by the consolidation script.
