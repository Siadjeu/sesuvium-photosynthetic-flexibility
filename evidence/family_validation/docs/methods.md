# Methods: identification and evidence-based reconciliation of photosynthesis-related gene families

## Main manuscript text

Photosynthesis-related gene families were examined across eight project datasets (AT, BV, MC, PO, SLP_Hap1, SP_Hex, SP_Tet and SS), using the 23 categories reported in Supplemental Table S6 of Wang et al. (2023; doi:10.1093/plphys/kiad451) as a reporting framework. Candidate membership was assessed independently of the published counts; Wang counts were not used as expected totals or acceptance targets. Protein provenance and annotation identifiers were reconciled before integrating family-level results. Each reported entry was keyed by family, species dataset and gene locus, with the associated protein identifier retained to support downstream analyses. Project AT denotes Amaranthus tricolor and was not treated as Arabidopsis thaliana. Species and haplotype codes were retained to avoid unsupported cross-study taxon equivalences.

Family-specific validation combined available sequence-similarity evidence, profile-HMM/domain architecture, alignments to positive and comparator references, and phylogenetic placement where reference coverage permitted. Bounded annotation reviews examined exceptional models using available protein, coding-sequence and genomic evidence. Potential truncations, inconsistent models and unresolved genomic disruptions were retained as explicit limitations rather than repaired without independent evidence. Existing completed analyses were reused during reconciliation; the final reporting step did not repeat sequence searches or tree inference.

Evidence was integrated into seven mutually exclusive categories for each family–species–locus record: supported, model caveat, limited-reference-panel caveat, partial, unresolved, outside the supported family set, and excluded. The primary table reports only supported records. Model-caveat and limited-panel records are reported separately for sensitivity analyses. Partial and unresolved candidates were retained without contributing to the primary supported counts. A zero denotes no records in a specified evidence category, not established genomic absence. Families lacking a resolved counting definition were reported as unavailable (NA). Neither family membership nor phylogenetic association alone was interpreted as evidence of a C4- or CAM-specific biochemical role, and the resulting tables were not treated as exhaustive genome-wide family counts.

The final reporting snapshot was generated from a checksum-pinned integrated ledger. Automated checks enforced unique family–species–locus keys, verified evidence-category totals and reconciled every species-by-family supported count against the ledger. Original gene and protein identifiers were exported in separate lists for each family, species and selected evidence tier. The evidence snapshot, scripts, provenance records and manuscript text were versioned together with SHA256 checksums.

## Supplementary methodological details

### Scope of the combined final-family validation

The combined workflow audited NAD-ME, NADP-ME, MDH, ASPAT, AlaAT, PEPCK, BASS2, MEP, MPC and PPA. Reference roles and shared candidate pools were tracked explicitly; related malic enzymes, MDH/LDH and aminotransferases were not treated as independent nonoverlapping discovery pools. MEP and PPA remained definition-blocked. The methods below apply specifically to the combined late-stage workflow and must not be interpreted as universal thresholds for all earlier family analyses. Earlier family-specific command histories and decisions are retained in `archive/`.

Recorded screening commands used DIAMOND with very-sensitive searching, an E-value ceiling of 1e-3, unrestricted target reporting and one HSP per query–target pair. These settings describe candidate retrieval, not sufficient criteria for membership. Pfam domains were assessed with HMMER using model-specific gathering thresholds (`--cut_ga`); the database and corrected-proteome hashes are retained in the provenance records. Positive/comparator roles were attached to reference identifiers before phylogenetic assessment.

For the combined core alignments, each designated HMM was required to have a single qualifying hit covering at least 80% of its model length, with domains in canonical, nonoverlapping order. This criterion established alignment eligibility and did not demonstrate complete gene models. Domain blocks were aligned separately with MAFFT (`--auto`, four threads and `--threadit 0`). Columns were retained when occupancy was at least 50% both among references and among all aligned tips, and retained domain blocks were concatenated. A minimum post-trimming per-tip occupancy of 50% was required. Partial and architecture-exception sequences remained recorded outside the principal core alignment.

The designated cores were PF01758 for BASS, PF00390/PF03949 for malic enzymes, PF00056/PF02866 for MDH/LDH, PF00155 for the domain-compatible aminotransferase subset, PF01293 for PEPCK and PF03650 for MPC. Alternative domain folds were not automatically excluded from the biological family simply because they were not represented by a particular core alignment.

Four completed unrooted core phylogenies (BASS, malic enzymes, MDH/LDH and aminotransferases) were inferred with IQ-TREE 3.1.3 using a fixed LG+G4 model, seed 17272, 1,000 ultrafast bootstrap replicates, 1,000 SH-aLRT replicates, `-bnni` and retention of identical sequences. The fixed model was used for bounded comparison and was not selected by a model-fit search. The interrupted aminotransferase analysis was resumed from its unchanged checkpoint with the same alignment and analysis settings. PEPCK and MPC received alignment-level evidence only because the available panels did not support a sufficiently discriminating specificity tree.

For the combined ledger consolidation, unrooted edge sides containing target positive references and no alternative reference tips were considered. The largest eligible side associated with each anchor was chosen before applying the support criterion of SH-aLRT ≥80 and UFBoot ≥95. Subsets of target-family references could define separate associations; monophyly of all family anchors was not assumed. Architecture flags were preserved. Failure to meet the support criterion, absence from a core alignment or incomplete comparator sampling did not automatically establish nonmembership. Conflicting paired-family supported assignments were checked before integration.

### Annotation limitations and source clarification

The earlier PPDK/PPDK-RP, V-PPase, V-ATPase, PPCK, PEPC, beta-CA, carrier, DIT, ALMT, TDT and PPT workstreams retain their own validation records. Targeted protein–CDS and protein-to-genome checks were used where available. Suitable read evidence was not available to resolve all suspected disruptions; these cases remained limited rather than being presented as confirmed biological loss or pseudogenization.

Wang Supplemental Table S6 supplied the reporting categories. The available Figure S9 legend clarified MEP as a proton:pyruvate cotransporter but did not establish the exact counted reference membership. PPA was listed separately from V_Ppase, without a sufficiently resolved inclusion rule for this project. A subsequent source-clarification collection recovered no counted-gene identifier leads and left the integrated ledger unchanged. Both families therefore remained NA. Published BV and PO counts were retained only as contextual comparisons; differences in genome versions, annotation and inclusion rules preclude direct expansion/loss conclusions.

### Reproducibility and provenance

`build_family_tables.py` uses the Python 3 standard library and regenerates tab-separated matrices, complete and tier-specific gene lists, per-family/per-species identifier files and QC from the frozen ledger. It does not rerun upstream biological analyses. The Excel workbook is a presentation snapshot; the reproducible canonical outputs are TSV files. Archived validation scripts are historical stage-specific records and require their original external inputs and environment; their presence is not a claim of a standalone end-to-end workflow. Software versions and database hashes are retained where observed rather than inferred for unlogged runs. The original source hash and any path-redacted released hash are both recorded in `provenance/source_inventory.tsv`.
