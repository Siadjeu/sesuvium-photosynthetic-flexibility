Many external references are partial sequences. Seventeen sequences in the
main alignment and eighteen in the sensitivity alignment exceeded 50% gaps or
ambiguity according to IQ-TREE. The main tree also reported one near-zero
internal branch. Identical topology across these two column masks does not
establish all branches or subtypes as well supported, nor does it test every
plausible alignment, taxon-sampling or evolutionary-model choice.

The historical KJ161651 species-metadata conflict remains documented; this
sequence was retained as a comparator, not an eligible classification anchor.
Phylogenetic subtype assignment alone does not establish C4 or CAM biochemical
function, tissue-specific activity, or physiological recruitment. Provisional
and unresolved assignments must retain their qualifiers in downstream work.
Identical proteins at distinct SS loci do not justify merging genomic loci.

Earlier reference-dependent tree and subtype conclusions are superseded by
this corrected assessment. Earlier validated locus counts remain unchanged.
This release closes the bounded correction/review; it does not claim that all
subtypes have been resolved. Additional biological validation is outside scope.
