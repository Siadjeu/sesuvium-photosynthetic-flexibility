Corrected PEPC phylogeny and subtype results

The corrected panel comprised 81 tips: 49 focal-species protein representatives
covering 51 validated loci and 32 reference sequences. All 18 historical KJ
references passed exon-aware translation verification. The translation error
affected historical PEPC1E1 references and all three PEPC1E2 references; the
three PEPC2 proteins were unchanged. KJ161576.1 regained sufficient focal-site
coverage after correction. AH004091-RA, Zm00001d024980_P001 and
Zm00001d037294_P001 were excluded for insufficient focal-site coverage.
All 49 focal representatives covered positions 519, 780 and 890.

The main and sensitivity alignments contained 965 and 1206 columns. The trees
shared all 78 nontrivial unrooted splits (Robinson-Foulds distance zero), although
support values differed. Of the 51 loci, 9 had supported
subtype assignments, 27 were provisional and
15 remained unresolved.

In SS, g29856, g11076 and g9483 were provisionally associated with PEPC1E2.
The enclosing group had SH-aLRT/UFBoot 99.6/94 in the main tree and 99.2/95
in the sensitivity tree. The predefined support threshold was not relaxed.
g7519 and g17009 were provisionally associated with PEPC2, with respective
group support 37.2/55 and 35.3/59. g2566, g32916 and g4086 remained unresolved.
These subtype categories do not change the validated PEPC locus counts.
