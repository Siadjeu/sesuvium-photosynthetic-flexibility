Maximum-likelihood phylogeny of validated PEPC protein representatives from
eight focal species and external references. The main tree contains 81 tips,
including 49 representatives of 51 focal-species loci and 32 references.
Species/accession labels and annotation tracks identify taxa and provisional
or supported subtype associations. Displayed five-residue windows are mapped
to maize Q84KR7 positions 519, 780 and 890 (two residues on each side).
Branch labels are SH-aLRT/UFBoot support. The tree is unrooted; any graphical
rerooting is for presentation unless justified separately. Identical SS protein
pairs are represented by g29856 (also g11076) and g7519 (also g17009).
The sensitivity tree has the same unrooted topology but different support.
