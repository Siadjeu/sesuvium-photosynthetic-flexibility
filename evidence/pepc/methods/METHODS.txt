PEPC reference correction and phylogenetic subtype assessment

Previously validated PEPC loci from eight focal species were represented by
49 protein sequences corresponding to 51 loci. Two pairs of SS loci encoding
identical proteins were represented once per pair in the trees while retaining
all loci in the counting ledger. Historical nucleotide-derived references were
reconstructed from annotated GenBank CDS features, respecting exon joins,
strand and codon_start. Computed translations were checked against annotated
proteins; partial terminal codons were accepted only when ambiguous completion
reproduced the annotated residue. All 18 KJ records passed these checks.
Other historical reference proteins were reconciled with their source FASTA
records. Previously excluded references were reconsidered.

MAFFT --auto aligned the protein panel. Reference coordinates corresponded to
ungapped maize Q84KR7. Sequences were retained when at least two of positions
519, 780 and 890 contained standard amino acids, with predefined exceptions
for SS g29856 and g7519. Both exceptions independently covered all three sites.
Five-residue windows were extracted by separately mapping positions p-2 to p+2.
Main alignment columns required standard residues in at least half of retained
focal representatives and at least one reference. The sensitivity alignment
retained columns containing at least one standard amino acid.

IQ-TREE 3.1.3 inferred unrooted maximum-likelihood trees, with ModelFinder BIC
selection, 1000 ultrafast bootstrap replicates with -bnni, and 1000 SH-aLRT
replicates. Main and sensitivity models were Q.PLANT+R4 and
Q.PLANT+I+R4, respectively. Identical tips were preserved.

Supported subtype assignments required concordant exclusive reference-group
placement in both trees, all retained eligible anchors of that subtype in the
enclosing group, at least two anchors from two species, SH-aLRT >=80 and
UFBoot >=95 in both trees. Concordant weaker associations were provisional;
other loci were unresolved. Historical metadata conflicts were excluded from
anchor eligibility. Assignment refers to phylogenetic subtype, not proven
photosynthetic function. Detailed algorithms accompany this release.
