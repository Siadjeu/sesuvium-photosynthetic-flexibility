Historical nucleotide-derived references are corrected before any phylogeny.
All 18 KJ accession.version records (12 PEPC1E1, 3 PEPC1E2, 3 PEPC2) must have
one translated CDS, supported local exon coordinates, genetic code 1 and no
translation exception. Joined exons are extracted in biological order;
complement locations are reverse complemented; codon_start is applied.
The computed protein must exactly match the GenBank /translation qualifier.
An annotated partial terminal codon can be padded with ambiguous N bases for
verification: it is accepted only if the resulting residue exactly matches
the annotated residue. Ambiguous codons resolving to one amino acid are
translated as that amino acid; otherwise X. Internal stops are never replaced
to manufacture a protein. Any KJ verification failure stops before inference.
The annotated translation is the protein used for verified KJ references.

Other historical Phytozome reference IDs are NOT treated as NCBI accessions.
They require a unique original amino-acid record in the two known original
FASTA files. Nucleotide-only or ambiguous sources are excluded and recorded.
An original protein FASTA validates provenance, not external biological
function. The four Arabidopsis protein comparators and Q84KR7 coordinate
anchor are retained from the checksummed predecessor protein release.
Previously coverage-excluded references are reconsidered if source-verified.

MAFFT --auto aligns current supported protein representatives and verified
references. Original gene/accession IDs are used throughout. Coordinates use
ungapped maize Q84KR7, 970 residues, positions 519, 780 and 890. Coverage means
one of the 20 standard amino acids at the mapped column; X/gaps are missing.
Retention requires >=2/3 positions, with explicit exceptions SS g29856 and
g7519. Five-residue windows map each of the five maize residues separately.
The two pairs of identical SS proteins remain represented once in the tree,
but all 51 validated loci remain in the locus table. Coverage-excluded focal
loci are recorded unresolved, not deleted from counts.

Main alignment retains columns with standard residues in at least half the
retained focal representatives and at least one reference. Sensitivity
alignment retains columns containing at least one standard residue. These
masks do not guarantee alignment accuracy; inspect fragment placement and
branch support. IQ-TREE ModelFinder selects an amino-acid model independently
for each alignment; 1000 UFBoot replicates with -bnni and 1000 SH-aLRT replicates
are used, with fixed seeds 519780 and 519890. -keep-ident preserves explicitly
retained identical tips. Trees are unrooted; no biological outgroup is imposed.

Subtype anchors require verified CDS, an explicit historical S7 subtype,
matching GenBank gene subtype, and no original species metadata conflict.
Public organism labels are displayed while historical labels are preserved.
KJ161651 remains ineligible as an anchor pending its metadata conflict review.
For every internal unrooted edge, both sides are considered. A candidate
association requires subtype-pure reference membership; contradictory
historical PEPC subtype references block that group. Supported assignment
requires the same unique subtype in both trees, all retained eligible anchors
for that subtype in the enclosing group, >=2 anchors from >=2 species, and
SH-aLRT >=80 plus UFBoot >=95 on both enclosing edges. Concordant weaker
associations are provisional; other cases are unresolved. These are operational
reference-guided assignments, not experimental C4/CAM function validation.
Historical labels lacking sufficient retained anchors cannot establish a
supported subtype. Loss of partial references after correction is reported,
not circumvented by relaxation of the user's coverage rule.

Earlier releases remain unchanged. Their reference-dependent tree/subtype
conclusions require replacement/reassessment using this corrected release.
No gene-family validation, RNA-seq, synteny or sequence annotation is rerun.

Local validation: all 12 supplied real GenBank PEPC1E1 records reproduce their
annotated translations with the exon-aware verifier. Location-parser checks
include reverse complement and rejection of unsupported remote/order locations.
An end-to-end test with synthetic additional records and mocked MAFFT/IQ-TREE
checks data flow, 51-locus accounting, original-ID exports and checksums.
Actual MAFFT/IQ-TREE inference and the six additional public record checks
will run on the HPC; no new biological results are claimed by this package.
