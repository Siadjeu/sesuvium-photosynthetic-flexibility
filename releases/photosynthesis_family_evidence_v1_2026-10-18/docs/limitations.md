# Limitations and unresolved questions retained at freeze

1. The supported counts are conservative evidence counts, not exhaustive family inventories or confirmed C4/CAM-specific functional genes.
2. MEP exact counted membership and PPA scope remain unresolved; both are NA. No reference definition was inferred solely to fill a table.
3. Model and panel caveats remain separate. Suitable read evidence was unavailable for resolving every suspected gene-model disruption.
4. The combined late-stage trees used a bounded reference panel and fixed LG+G4 model. Support thresholds were not relaxed to obtain desired assignments.
5. The 80 ASPAT support-limited candidates remain unresolved; the UFBoot value of 74 did not pass the criterion of 95.
6. Zero supported PEPCK/MPC/PPT counts do not establish absence. Their caveated records remain available.
7. Shared screening pools mean family totals cannot be summed as a nonredundant genome-wide gene count. Ploidy and assembly representation further preclude naive expansion/loss interpretation.
8. Project species codes must remain attached to IDs. Exact taxon, accession and haplotype labels should be checked against the original source manifest before manuscript submission.
9. This release reproduces reporting from the frozen evidence ledger. A complete end-to-end rerun requires external genomic inputs, reference databases and recorded environments.
10. Historical checkpoint decisions are retained for audit and may be superseded by the final integrated ledger. Use the latter for this release's counts.
