# Results: a reconciled, tiered inventory of photosynthesis-related family loci

Reconciliation of the family-specific analyses produced 3,193 unique family–species–locus evidence records across eight project datasets. Integrated evidence was available for 21 of the 23 Wang Table S6 categories. The ledger contained 657 supported records, 71 records with model caveats, 285 with limited-reference-panel caveats, 99 partial records, 627 unresolved records, 1,414 records outside the supported family set and 40 excluded records. These totals describe family-assigned evidence records, not a nonredundant total of C4/CAM genes across families, because related families can share screening pools.

The supported set included 45 beta-CA, 51 PEPC, 27 PPCK, 26 NAD-ME, 42 NADP-ME, 94 MDH, 12 ASPAT, 15 AlaAT, 15 PPDK, 14 PPDK-RE, 11 BASS2, 17 DIT2, 17 DIC, 41 DTC, 95 ALMT, 10 TDT, 85 V_ATPase and 40 V_Ppase records. These family totals pool the eight datasets and do not establish differences in gene-family size between species. Species-specific supported counts and the corresponding original gene and protein identifiers are provided in the accompanying tables.

Uncertainty was concentrated in several distinct classes. ALMT retained 32 model-caveat records in addition to its 95 supported records. V_ATPase retained 23 model-caveat and 208 limited-panel records alongside 85 supported records, illustrating the importance of subunit-specific evidence. PEPCK and MPC retained 12 and 40 limited-panel records, respectively, while PPT retained 25 limited-panel records and one model-caveat record. Consequently, zeros for these three families in the supported-only table do not indicate their absence from the genomes.

In the bounded aminotransferase review, 80 ASPAT-associated candidates remained unresolved because their reference-associated edge had SH-aLRT support of 91.6 but UFBoot support of 74, below the applied UFBoot criterion of 95. Of these candidates, 78 lacked the recorded architecture flags and two retained architecture flags. Domain compatibility was therefore not used to override insufficient phylogenetic support. These 80 records form a subset of the broader unresolved ASPAT evidence rather than a separate addition to the ledger.

MEP and PPA remained unavailable for numerical reporting. Source review clarified the MEP label but did not recover the exact counted reference membership, while the scope of PPA remained unresolved. The subsequent source-clarification step recovered no gene-identifier leads and made no changes to the evidence ledger. Both categories were retained as NA rather than assigned inferred values.

All 184 species-by-family cells in the supported reporting matrix reconciled with the locus-level ledger, and no duplicate family–species–locus keys were present. The combined late-stage consolidation reported no paired-family supported-assignment conflicts. The frozen release therefore provides a traceable conservative set for downstream analysis, together with separate caveated and unresolved sets. It does not establish exhaustive family membership, C4/CAM-specific biochemical function, genome-wide absence, or gene-family expansion caused by whole-genome duplication.

## Suggested table legends

**Table Sx. Supported photosynthesis-related family loci across eight project datasets.** Columns follow the 23 categories in Wang et al. Supplemental Table S6; rows correspond to project species/assembly codes. Values count unique family–species–locus records assigned to the supported evidence tier. NA denotes an unresolved family definition or unavailable integrated evidence. Zero denotes no supported records and does not establish genomic absence. Caveated counts are reported separately. AT denotes Amaranthus tricolor.

**Table Sy. Locus-level evidence and identifiers for downstream analyses.** Each row specifies a family, species dataset, original gene locus, protein identifier, evidence category, count tier, source and model/scope limitation. The primary downstream set contains the 657 supported family–locus records. Model-caveat and limited-panel sets are retained separately; partial, unresolved, outside-family-set and excluded records remain available for audit. Records must be joined using species and original identifiers, without stripping prefixes or counting protein isoforms as additional loci.
