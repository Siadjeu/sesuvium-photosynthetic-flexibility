# Photosynthesis-family evidence report

This report summarizes locus-level evidence categories. It is not an exhaustive count of C4/CAM-functional genes or an exact reproduction of Wang TableS6.

## Counting convention

Each family/species/locus key appears once. Supported, model-caveat, limited-panel, partial and unresolved categories remain separate. Outside-family-set and excluded records are provided separately. Zero means no records in that evidence category; it does not establish genomic absence. NA means the family lacks an integrated ledger. The compact matrix cell order is supported / model caveat / limited-panel caveat / partial / unresolved. Do not sum across families: shared screening pools and possible multifunctionality make such totals non-unique.
