Discovery rules use a 20-bit allowance below the weakest supported profile score OR a PPCK panel hit within 20 bits of the best comparison hit (E<=1e-5). These are permissive prioritization heuristics, not family acceptance/rejection thresholds.
Comparison panel consists of 98 non-PPCK-group tree candidates and four kinase controls, not an exhaustive kinase classification.
No-trigger candidates are not excluded; short/divergent/missing gene models may be missed. Queue entries require review and cannot increase counts automatically.
