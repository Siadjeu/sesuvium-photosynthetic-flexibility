Bounded consistency check of 23 V-ATPase exceptions. Standard nuclear genetic code, supplied CDS orientation and reading frame; no frame search or sequence repair. Source proteins are compared to screened proteins after case normalization and removal of at most one terminal stop; the source length and normalization status are recorded. Screened proteins are preserved unchanged in the output. One terminal stop is allowed on either protein or translation; internal stops and incomplete codons remain flagged. Ambiguous codons are X and explicitly flagged even if matching protein X.
AT CDS recovery uses exact IDs from mapped transcript/CDS GFF attributes and original FASTA record IDs or bracketed protein_id/transcript_id/ID/Name fields. Multiple matching records remain unresolved, even if their sequences are identical. Full matched headers and original source checksum are retained.
An exact protein-CDS match shows internal consistency of annotation products, not independent support for a correct gene model, fusion, completeness, enzyme activity or C4/CAM function. No genome translation, new alignment, pseudogene call or gene-model repair is performed. Existing domain/alignment caveats and distinct loci are preserved.
