Targeted evidence collection only. No gene repair, isoform replacement, acceptance or counting.
GFF coordinates remain original 1-based inclusive. FASTA windows use genomic plus orientation; interval table is 0-based half-open.
Annotations include neighboring features in the windows. No sequence-ID renaming or fuzzy matching is performed.
FOUND does not certify that every expected isoform was recovered: compare mapping and sequence-match tables.
Missing sources or exact matches are reported; COMPLETE means collection finished, not biological validation.
Source paths are relative to PROJECT_ROOT. Full input genomes are not copied.
