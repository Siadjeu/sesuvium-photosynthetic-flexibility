Exploratory protein-to-genome alignments; no annotation replacement or final counts.
14 supported candidate queries plus 2 reference queries and 3 flagged-model controls.
All queries searched against each window. Flagged controls are not independent rescue evidence.
Local GFF coordinates refer to WINDOW, genomic GFF coordinates to original chromosomes (1-based inclusive).
Original annotation excerpts can contain only part of neighboring genes. Neighbor overlap column means predicted CDS overlaps a neighboring gene span, not necessarily its CDS.
Target overlap uses CDS intervals, not the intron-containing alignment span.
Extension sizes are descriptive and meaningful only for target-associated alignments; they do not establish gene repair.
Query coverage is aligned query span, not identical-residue coverage. Missing GFF attributes are NA, not zero.
Maximum intron is 20 kb; windows have only 5 kb flanks. No hit is not absence; edge hits may require larger windows.
Raw residue alignments and logs are retained. Proposed models may reflect paralogs or alignment artifacts.
