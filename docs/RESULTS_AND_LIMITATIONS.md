# Results and interpretation limits

These are integrated existing results; publication preparation performs no new
sequence, evolutionary or differential-expression analysis.

| Evidence | Supported-subset result | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| WGD/segmental | 465 of 657 loci | MCScanX class does not distinguish a whole-genome event from segmental duplication |
| CAFE5 primary expansion | Nine PO PEPC loci in OG0000986 | One expanded orthogroup, not nine independent significant tests |
| CAFE5 primary branch assessment | 23 supported loci had a primary branch test | Model-table presence is not branch testing |
| CAFE5 robust expansion | Not assessable for this subset | No sensitivity-model branch tests among the 629 loci present in the comparison table; do not report absence of expansion |
| Positive selection | Six significant loci among 296 assessed | Valid supplied aBSREL tests with existing global BH q<0.05; no subset-specific FDR recalculation |
| PO DE | 94 loci among 114 assessable, 127 supported total | Union over registered contrasts; not automatically drought-responsive or CAM-regulated |
| SS DE | 52 loci among 83 assessable, 99 supported total | Same distinction between untested/unassessable and tested nonsignificant |

The six positive-selection loci are MC MDH `gene_17069`; PO V_ATPase
`evm.TU.LG04.521`; PO ALMT `evm.TU.LG19.1304` and `evm.TU.LG21.335`;
SP_Hex MDH `gene_Spor0079650.1`; and SS PPCK `g20509`.
BV and SLP_Hap1 were background-only in the supplied selection analysis. No
supported AT locus had a valid supplied test. These are scope limits, not
negative tests. Thirteen loci lack assigned orthogroups despite exact gene-ID
mapping and duplication evidence.

DE reporting uses within-contrast adjusted P<0.05 and absolute apeglm-shrunken
log2FC >=1. Pooled-contrast adjustment is retained separately as sensitivity
information. The registered numerator/denominator controls each effect's sign.

The wider family evidence ledger has 3,193 records across evidence tiers; the
657 supported loci are a subset. MEP and PPA definition gaps and model/partial/
limited-panel caveats remain visible. The tables do not establish exhaustive
formal genome-wide C4/CAM gene counts or locus-specific photosynthetic function.

The corrected PEPC result is documented in evidence/pepc/results. All 49 focal
representatives cover the three maize sites. The SS PEPC1E2 association remains
provisional (main UFBoot 94; sensitivity 95); thresholds were not relaxed.
Many external references are partial. Stable topology under two column masks
does not establish all branches, functions or subtype assignments.

SS metabolite analysis uses relative abundance for a predefined six-organic-acid
panel. Three cotyledon and six leaf plants were measured during daytime. RNA-seq
used different plants. No within-individual gene–metabolite correlation or diel
CAM cycling can be inferred. Normalization/detection-limit processing and
identification confidence remain incompletely documented. Full limitations and
statistical methods accompany the metabolite evidence.
